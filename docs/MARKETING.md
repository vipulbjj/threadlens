## LinkedIn post

**Title hook (first line):** I built a free tool to help couples (and friends) understand their chat history before the hard conversation.

**Post body:**

Most relationship fights aren’t about one message—they’re about patterns we feel but can’t name.

So I shipped **ThreadLens** — a free, private web app that turns a WhatsApp / Telegram / iMessage export into something you can actually discuss:

→ Who carries the thread (balance, not blame)  
→ Conflict & repair signals in text  
→ Guided prompts like “what keeps triggering our fights?”  
→ **Parsing stays in your browser** — no account, no upload of your full chat for analytics  

Optional AI runs on **Groq’s free tier** when configured (not your whole export—only what you ask about).

**Use cases I’m seeing:**
- Couples prepping for a repair conversation  
- Friends checking if a group chat feels one-sided  
- Family threads, work boundaries, personal reflection  

It’s a reach / craft project, not therapy or legal advice. If it helps one couple pause before sending the text they’ll regret, it did its job.
